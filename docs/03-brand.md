## Positioning

- **For** Australian property investors, share investors, SMSF trustees and business owners
- **who** need to know what the 2026-27 tax shake-up means for them,
- **Taxshift is** a daily-checked, plain-English tracker
- **that** separates law from proposal and shows who wants to change it back.
- **Unlike** adviser blogs and news, it is neutral, sourced line by line and never selling anything.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #0A0A0A | Text, heavy rules, "in force" stamps |
| Paper | #FFFFFF | Background |
| Shift magenta | #E5007D | The single accent: change, risk, "law coming", repeal pledges |
| Rule grey | #D9D9D9 | Hairlines |
| Muted | #4A4A4A | Secondary text (9:1 on white) |

- Magenta on white is 4.8:1 (AA for text). In dark mode it lifts to #FF4FA8 on #0C0C0C (6.5:1).
- Party colours (Labor red, Liberal blue, Greens green, One Nation orange, teal) are never used anywhere. Neutrality is a brand rule.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Big Shoulders Display 900 | 56–260px |
| Display numbers (big stats) | Big Shoulders Display 900 | 72–200px |
| Headings | Big Shoulders Display 900, uppercase | 30–120px |
| Body | Public Sans 400/600/800 | 17px / 1.6 |
| Labels, dates, data | DM Mono 500, uppercase, +0.08em | 12–15px |

Public Sans was designed for government information: it signals "official facts", the display face signals "front page".

## Status language (shape, not just colour)

| Status | Stamp | Look |
|---|---|---|
| In force | IN FORCE | Solid ink block |
| Law, not started | LAW | Magenta outline |
| Passed, awaiting assent | PASSED | Magenta outline |
| Proposal | PROPOSED | Dashed ink outline |
| Dead | DROPPED / LAPSED / REPEALED | Grey, struck through |

## Voice

| We are | Sounds like | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Blunt | "Gains after 1 July 2027 lose the 50% discount." | "Exciting changes are coming!" |
| Neutral | "Coalition pledges repeal." | "Labor's cash grab", "Coalition's reckless plan" |
| Sourced | "Per the Act and ATO guidance…" | "Experts say…" |
| Helpful, not advice | "Keep contract dates." | "You should sell now." |

## Imagery

- No stock photos, no illustrations of houses or coins. Type, numbers and rules are the imagery.
- Icons are text glyphs from a fixed set (■ ✓ ✕ ↺ ▲ ≈ –), always paired with a word.
- Social image: `public/og.png` (regenerate with `python scripts/make-og.py`).
